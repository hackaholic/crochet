# Pricing strategy

## Position

Crochet Bloom should compete as a premium, hand-finished gift brand. Price must cover skilled making time, premium materials, packaging, platform and payment fees, wastage, tax, and a sustainable margin. Do not compete with mass-produced or heavily discounted marketplace listings.

## SKU pricing method

For every product, record:

1. Materials and consumables cost.
2. Making, assembly, finishing, and quality-check time.
3. Artisan hourly rate.
4. Packaging and expected shipping subsidy.
5. Payment/platform fees and tax.
6. Margin for operating cost, returns, and growth.

Use this as the floor price:

```text
floor = materials + packaging + shipping subsidy + fees + (hours × artisan hourly rate)
retail = floor + sustainable operating margin
```

Customisation, urgent dispatch, gift wrapping, and add-on cards should be priced as visible add-ons, not absorbed silently into the base price.
